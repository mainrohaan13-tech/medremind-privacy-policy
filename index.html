Privacy Policy for MedRemind

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Developer: Syed Hamza
Package Name: com.hamzaapps.medicationreminder

1. Overview

MedRemind ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline-first medication reminder and schedule tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal health information.

2. Data Storage & Collection

MedRemind does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or medical data on external servers. All medication names, dosages, schedules, stock counts, and app settings are stored exclusively on your local device.

3. Data Export & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export: You may export your medication records as a JSON file using the "Export Medication Data (JSON)" option in Settings.
  • Delete: You may permanently delete all stored records from your device at any time using the "Delete All Data" option in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Device Permissions

MedRemind requests the following optional system permissions strictly to deliver timely medication alerts:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To alert you when it is time to take your scheduled medication.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): To ensure medication reminders trigger accurately at your chosen time.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption: Optional setting to prevent your device from delaying background reminders.

5. Third-Party Services & Ads

MedRemind contains no advertisements, no third-party analytics trackers, and requires no user account or sign-in.

6. Medical Disclaimer

MedRemind is a personal scheduling aid and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding your medications.
