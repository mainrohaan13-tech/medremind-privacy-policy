Privacy Policy for MedRemind

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Developer: Syed Hamza
Application Name: MedRemind
Package Name: com.hamzaapps.medicationreminder

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

MedRemind ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline-first medication reminder, dosage schedule, and refill tracking application. Your privacy and the confidentiality of your personal health routines are our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use MedRemind, how it is stored, and what rights you have over your data.

2. Local Medication & Health Data Storage

MedRemind is designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not require user registration, accounts, or cloud synchronization, and we do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or health-related records on external servers. All information you enter into the app—including:

  • Medication names, dosage amounts, forms, and intake instructions;
  • Reminder schedules, frequency intervals, snooze logs, and adherence statistics;
  • Inventory stock counts, low-stock thresholds, and app theme/language preferences;

remains stored exclusively in private local storage on your Android device.

3. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Because your medication records remain on your device, you maintain complete ownership and control over how long your data is kept:

  • Retention: Your records remain on your device only for as long as you choose to keep them or until you uninstall the application.
  • Export & Backup: You may export a local backup copy of your medication records in JSON format at any time using the "Export Medication Data (JSON)" feature in Settings.
  • Permanent Deletion: You may permanently erase all saved medications, schedules, and adherence logs immediately using the "Delete All Data" option in Settings, clearing the app's storage in Android system settings, or uninstalling MedRemind.

4. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

MedRemind requests only the system permissions necessary to deliver reliable medication reminders and display advertisements:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display timely medication and low-stock refill alerts on your device.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to ensure scheduled medication alerts trigger accurately at your chosen reminder times.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Optional setting that prevents Android's battery saver from delaying or suppressing scheduled dose alarms.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used to provide tactile alert feedback when a reminder triggers (controllable in Settings).
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to request, load, and display advertisements through Google AdMob.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

MedRemind uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display banner and app-open advertisements so the app can remain free to use. While your medication and health entries are never shared with AdMob or any third party, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain technical information to serve, personalize (where permitted), and measure advertisements, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID);
  • IP address (used to estimate general geographic region);
  • Basic device specifications, crash/performance diagnostics, and ad interaction events (impressions and clicks).

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads. For full details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Data Security

All medication and schedule data saved in MedRemind resides within your device's sandboxed application storage, protected by your Android operating system's built-in security controls and device lock screen. We recommend keeping your device protected with a PIN, password, or biometric lock and storing any exported JSON backup files in a secure location.

7. Children's Privacy

MedRemind is intended for general audiences and adult caregivers managing personal or family medication schedules. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any support communication, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

8. Medical Disclaimer

MedRemind is a personal organization and reminder tool only. It does not provide medical advice, clinical diagnosis, prescription management, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a qualified physician, pharmacist, or healthcare provider regarding your medications, dosages, and medical decisions, and never rely solely on any software application for life-critical medication timing.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements, regulatory updates, or changes in third-party SDK requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date at the top. Continued use of MedRemind after updates are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
